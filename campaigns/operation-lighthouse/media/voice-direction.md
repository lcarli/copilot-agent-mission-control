# Operation Lighthouse voice direction

## Shared direction

Use a calm, credible public-service delivery. Speakers communicate urgency
without panic, leave short pauses around evidence and safety instructions, and
never imitate military command, advertising, or synthetic assistant voices.
Record clean, dry dialogue at a consistent distance so narration remains
intelligible over the quiet command-center ambience.

## English

Use clear international English at approximately 135 words per minute. Preserve
the canonical names `Port Azure`, `Maya Chen`, `Jules Martin`, `Aurora`, and
`Mission Commander`. Emphasize evidence, coordination, safety, and human
approval without making promises of guaranteed outcomes.

## French

Use natural international French at approximately 130 words per minute. Avoid
literal English syntax and excessive anglicisms, while preserving canonical
proper names. Use an operational, inclusive register and articulate technical
terms consistently with the campaign glossary.

## Brazilian Portuguese

Use natural Brazilian Portuguese at approximately 135 words per minute. Prefer
clear operational language, inclusive plural constructions, and the campaign
glossary. Preserve canonical proper names and avoid exaggerated broadcast-news
intonation.
