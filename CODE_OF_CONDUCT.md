# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and inclusive community for people of
all backgrounds, identities, experience levels, languages, and abilities.
Participation in this project must support learning, constructive
collaboration, and professional respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing harmful impact directly.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without gatekeeping.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not personal judgments.
- Respect names, pronouns, language preferences, privacy, and boundaries.
- Credit contributions accurately.
- Use fictional or consented data in examples and demonstrations.
- Help keep workshops safe for beginners as well as experienced participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory language, imagery, jokes, or exclusion.
- Personal attacks, insults, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information, credentials, communications, or identifying
  event data without explicit permission.
- Encouraging unsafe emergency behavior or presenting fictional workshop output
  as real operational guidance.
- Deliberately undermining event isolation, other participants, or shared
  infrastructure.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in repository activity, reviews, discussions, project events,
workshops, demonstrations, and public spaces where a person represents the
project.

## Reporting

For immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service or event
venue staff.

For project conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately. If
no other private channel is available, use the repository's private
vulnerability reporting form and state that the report is a confidential
conduct concern. Do not identify affected people in a public issue.

Reports should include only the information needed to understand the concern:
what happened, where it happened, relevant dates, and any requested safety
measure. Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a private warning, limit
participation, remove a person from a workshop or project space, temporarily
suspend access, or permanently ban participation. The response will consider
severity, impact, context, prior behavior, and the safety of affected people.

Retaliation, repeated behavior, credible threats, disclosure of private
information, or attempts to compromise participant safety may result in
immediate removal.

Maintainers who have a conflict of interest must not lead the response. When
possible, reporters and affected people will receive an acknowledgement and a
summary of the resolution, subject to privacy and safety constraints.
